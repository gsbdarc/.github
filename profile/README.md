# Stanford GSB · Data, Analytics, and Research Computing

The **Data, Analytics, and Research Computing (DARC)** team supports research at the Stanford Graduate School of Business. We help researchers work with data, apply analytical methods, and use research computing infrastructure. DARC is part of the Stanford GSB Research Hub.

This organization shares research computing tools, tutorials, and examples developed by our team.

## Start here

- **[About DARC](https://darc.stanford.edu/)** — our team and research support services.
- **[RCpedia](https://rcpedia.stanford.edu/)** — documentation and guidance for research computing at Stanford GSB.
- **[Research computing and AI skills](https://github.com/gsbdarc/gsb-research-computing-ai-skills)** — hands-on materials covering the command line, the Yens cluster, Slurm, GPUs, and AI coding tools.
- **[Yens onboarding](https://github.com/gsbdarc/yens-onboarding-2026)** — a two-day introduction to research computing and AI at Stanford GSB.

## Tools and examples

- **[Ollama helper](https://github.com/gsbdarc/ollama_helper)** — run Ollama on Stanford computing clusters.
- **[vLLM helper](https://github.com/gsbdarc/vllm_helper)** — run vLLM on Stanford computing clusters.
- **[Qualtrics AI chatbot](https://github.com/gsbdarc/gsb-qualtrics-ai-chatbot)** — an AI chatbot embedded in a Qualtrics survey, deployed with Terraform.
- **[Stanford SAML full-stack starter](https://github.com/gsbdarc/saml-fullstack-stanford)** — a Stanford-authenticated Next.js + FastAPI app behind a Shibboleth SP.
- **[Bay Area smoke](https://github.com/gsbdarc/bay-area-smoke)** — seasonal wildfire-smoke risk built from EPA, NOAA and Stanford ECHOLab data.
